# Divergence register

Every deliberate difference between the Rust port and the C++ reference.
The owner reviews this register at each gate and signs it off in work
package P7.1. See [verification.md](verification.md) for when an entry is
required.

Reasons are one of: *undefined behaviour in the reference*, *legacy
platform dependence*, *numeric policy* (decision T11), *owner decision*
(cite its ID), or *technical decision* (cite its ID; added 2026-11-17).

The owner accepted D11 on 2026-11-17. On the same day the owner accepted
D87 to D117 and D150 to D156: the rows that replace undefined behaviour of
the reference as a class, and the design rows one by one (D87 to D93, D95,
D96, D98, D99, D107, D117 and D156).

The owner accepted D118 to D128 (P2.4) on 2026-11-17: D124 to D126 as open
gaps that P3.3 removes (turret aiming and gun lock may come with P2.5);
D122 and D123 to be revisited when multi-part ships and golems need them;
D120 removed before P2.4 merged, once the P2.1 `ClassifyPoint` was on
`main` (the ID is not reused); the rest as design choices.

The owner accepted D190 to D192 (P2.5) on 2026-11-18, as replacements of
undefined behaviour in the reference.

The owner accepted D160 to D163, D170 to D173 and D193 to D196 (P2.5) on
2026-11-18: D195 on the condition that the world delivers the queued
notifications after the tick in the order the reference delivers them
during it (done on `claude/p2.5-goto`); D174 removed (its gaps are open
items of the ledger's AI sub-row; the ID is not reused).

The owner accepted D180 to D183 (the shared remainder of P2.4) on
2026-11-18. On the same day D124 was removed (turret joints and the lock
clock are driven now; what is left is D183; the ID is not reused) and D128
was narrowed to the power pass.

ID ranges, reserved on 2026-11-18 so that work packages running in
parallel do not collide: IDs below D200 stay with Phases 2 and 3; D200 to
D299 belong to Phase 4. A work package takes the next free ID of its
range.

The owner accepted D200 and D201 (P4.14) on 2026-11-18, as replacements of
unspecified and undefined behaviour in the reference.

The owner accepted D202 to D205 (P4.3) on 2026-11-18: D203 and D204 as
replacements of undefined behaviour in the reference, D202 and D205 as
design choices.

The owner accepted D206 to D208 (P4.3), D220 and D222 to D229 (P4.10)
and D230 to D234 (P4.12, P4.13) on 2026-11-18, D220 and D224 as amended
by owner decision O26 (X3DAudio for 3D voices; reverb whatever `useEAX`

The owner accepted D240 to D244 (P4.14 Build) on 2026-11-18, as
replacements of undefined behaviour and defects in the reference and as
simplifications that change nothing for the shipped data.

The owner accepted D209 (P4.2) on 2026-11-18, keeping the fixed set of
static samplers of record 0004; a trace that shows an approximated stage

D260 to D264 (P4.4, first increment) are proposed and await the owner's

D245 to D249 (P4.14 run time: curves, spline paths, playback) are
