⚠ THE PROSE AND THE STATE MACHINE DISAGREED — RESOLVED 2026-08-08
Rev A8 decided order modality is intent-led: the context bar carries a Move/Attack/Ability toggle and long-press honours it, with hit-test surviving only as the Auto fallback. But §9.2's state machine still encoded pure hit-test — LongPress ▸ Wheel over an entity, LongPress ▸ Puck over empty space — with no toggle edge anywhere in it, so a frozen T-TEST-CLI-05 would have encoded whichever half its author read. Decided: intent-led, with the §1 edges written into the machine, and Move — not Auto — as the default. §1 is now freezable.

§3 THE COMMAND WHEEL §9.4 — eight sectors, contextual, illegal greyed before any round trip
▣12 SHIPSTALON WING
{{ w.icon }} {{ w.label }} {{ w.sub }}
HOLD IS THUMB-NEAREST — WHICH MEANS THE WHEEL ROTATES
§9.4 puts Hold in "the thumb-nearest sector by default", which is a rule with a hole in it: thumb-nearest depends on which hand and where on the screen the press landed. The wheel must therefore rotate so Hold sits on the arc closest to the holding thumb — down-left for a right-handed grip, down-right for left, and clamped toward centre when the press is near a screen edge. The other seven keep their relative order so the whole ring stays learnable as a shape rather than a lookup.

SUB-WHEELS CASCADE OUTWARD, NEVER REPLACE
A ▸ sector opens its children as a second ring outside the first, with the parent ring dimmed but still drawn. Replacing the ring would cost the player their place — they would have to re-read eight labels to find their way back — and U1 caps the depth at one level anyway, so there is never a third ring to run out of room for. Sliding back inside the parent ring cancels the cascade without committing, which makes the whole interaction recoverable without lifting the finger.
{{ m.text }}

Greying is not enough on its own. 02 §10's pre-check knows precisely why a sector is illegal, and a greyed label that will not say why is the most frustrating state in any command UI. The centre hub doubles as the reason line while a finger rests over an illegal sector — "Dock — no station in range" — which costs nothing, needs no round trip, and turns a dead end into an explanation.

U4 requires every round trip to have a ghost and every rejection a bounce and a reason. The reason string has one home — 07d §4's bounce toast — whether it came from the local pre-check (instant) or the server (a round trip later), and the two must be indistinguishable to the player. That is 07d's `BounceParity` gate, and it is the reason the pre-check exists at all: a locally-refused order should feel identical to a remotely-refused one, only faster.

§5 PROBE RINGS rev A7 — the sub-wheel's most interesting child, and the one geometry that is the readout
{{ p.text }}
NO NEW INTERACTION MODEL — THAT IS THE POINT
A probe is placed with an ordinary Move-shaped drag out of Abilities ▸, so the exploration loop reuses the puck wholesale. What is new is only the readout: each deployed probe draws a ring at its true disc radius, overlaps shade cumulatively, and an unsettled probe stays dashed until its dwell completes.
Pinch on a selected ring steps the radius through the eight-rung ladder — the touch-native form of the trade the ladder encodes: wider covers more and resolves less. A two-ring lens and a three-ring lens are visually distinct without a number, which is why planar trilateration was chosen over a signal-strength readout in the first place.
THE ONE THING THAT FOUGHT 07c
Probe rings are the single overlay class that survives to strategic zoom (07c §3), and cumulative overlap shading would make them order-dependent alpha — the one place in the overlay pass where draw order changes the image. Decided: a commutative blend, so the pass gains no sort stage for one class. Overlap count is the information, and a count has no order.
§6 WHAT THIS FREEZES, AND WHAT STILL NEEDS AN OWNER
DECIDED — T-TEST-CLI-05 MAY FREEZE THESE
▸ Intent-led modality, with the three missing state-machine edges written out in §1. Auto is a setting, not the default.
▸ The wheel rotates so Hold lands under the thumb; the other seven hold relative order.
▸ Sub-wheels cascade outward, parent dimmed and retained; sliding back inside cancels without committing.
▸ The hub carries the illegality reason from the 02 §10 pre-check while a finger rests on a greyed sector.
▸ The footprint is the real formation solve — one station tick per ship, never decorative.
DECIDED — 2026-08-08
✅ Handedness comes from a settings toggle only, never inference — a wheel that silently re-orders itself is worse than one that is merely mirrored. This promotes §4.10 settings from a nicety to a hard prerequisite of the wheel.
✅ Obstructed footprint snaps to nearest valid. Refusal punishes a gesture already committed; silent deformation changes the order without saying so. The snap is visible before release, so what is seen is what commits.
✅ Probe rings blend commutatively — overlap count is the information, and a count has no order. The overlay pass gains no sort stage for one class.
