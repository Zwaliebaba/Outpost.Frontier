07b's discipline, adapted from contacts to property: form language carries the family (raw ore is filled and irregular, refined alloy is outlined and regular), silhouette carries identity, and hue only assists — every glyph on this sheet must pass in grayscale, which is what lets the colour-vision palettes retune item hues freely without touching a single shape. Ore is the first item family, not the last (08c): the form rules below are written for the families that do not exist yet.

§1 FORM RULES four, and every future item family answers to them
RAW FILLED, REFINED OUTLINED
The family channel, read before identity: a mixed Bay sorts ore from alloy pre-attentively. Raw forms are irregular and asymmetric; manufactured forms are regular and symmetric — the same nothing-that-moves-is-symmetric logic 07b uses, inverted for things that were made.
SILHOUETTE CARRIES IDENTITY
Grayscale must pass — §5 proves it on this sheet. Hue is an assist, never the signal, so deutan/tritan palettes may reassign item hues as a table swap; the shapes never change. Same argument that made the flag rings colour-safe.
THE 20 PX FLOOR HOLDS
Below 20 px the glyph is refused (07b's floor), and the letter code stands in: FC · AC · NB. The CARGO manifest's column headers are that fallback already shipping — this sheet names the rule the tab was already obeying.
HUE BY MAJORITY INPUT
An alloy wears its majority ore's hue; a tie wears refined silver. The recipe is readable off the icon — Chromite Conduit is teal because it is mostly Nebulite — and a new recipe never needs a hue meeting, only arithmetic.
§2 THE ORES filled, irregular — hues picked clear of every semantic token on the same surface
FERRO-CHROMA FC
itemFerroChroma · #c08a52 · 300 L/unit
A faceted chunk of shattered crust — the bulk workhorse, so its form is the heaviest. Bronze at low chroma: the cheap ore looks cheap on purpose.
ASTRACITE AC
itemAstracite · #b99cff · 200 L/unit
A shard cluster — the only pointed silhouette in the family. Violet: the radiation ore, in the one hue band the semantic palette never uses.
NEBULITE NB
itemNebulite · #4fd6c0 · 250 L/unit
A porous nodule inside a dashed gas halo — the halo is the volatility, and it is the one ore glyph with a second element. Teal, kept well off the allied cyan by saturation and the halo geometry.

Hue collision audit, run against every token that can share a surface with a stack: bronze sits between hostile red and caution amber at half their chroma — separable from both with hue removed; violet occupies the palette's only unused band; teal is distinguished from allied #4de1ff by saturation, and allied never appears beside a manifest anyway — standings colour ships, not property. All three clear 4.5:1 against void.

Nova-Steel's tag is red on this sheet for the same reason its REFINERY row is: it is geography, never in High-Sec.

Where the hue applies: constellation zoom and closer, and the system view's field marker. The strategic map's region zoom keeps its phosphor-brightness pips — a 6 px pip cannot carry a hue honestly, and the map §5 already ruled region zoom says count and grade only. 07b's SITE glyph (amber dashed diamond, unresolved probe contact) is untouched: that diamond means "unknown thing here", these mean "known field of a known kind" — same family silhouette, deliberately.

§6 OPEN
NEEDS A DECISION
▸ Where the slot mapping lives. Proposed: iconAtlasSlot per item in Economy.json — the hull table's own precedent, and it keeps item vocabulary on the game's side of the seam. The alternative (a client-side table keyed by ItemTypeId) puts game words in the engine.
▸ Tie-silver vs authored hue. The majority rule prices Nova-Steel at plain silver; an owner who wants the endgame alloy to gleam overrides per-item — one field, but it breaks "recipe readable off the icon" and should be a ruling, not a drift.
▸ Wreck-loot surfaces. If alloys ever appear on tactical grids (§5b wrecks), item hue meets standings colour on one surface for the first time — the neutral-frame treatment needs a decision before combat, not during it.
GROUNDED
▸ Atlas discipline is 07b's: SDF, single channel, one source from 12 px to 96 px, residency Critical. Eleven new slots on the existing icon page — no new texture, no new pass, R9 untouched.
▸ Grayscale-pass means palette freedom: the three colour-vision palettes reassign item hues as table entries; no shape work is owed, matching the HudPalette posture ("a swap rather than a redesign").
▸ Every consuming surface pre-agreed the geometry: D-P2's manifest columns, D-P3's recipe rows and the map §5's diamonds were all drawn against this sheet's rules before it existed — this document closes the loop rather than opening one.
