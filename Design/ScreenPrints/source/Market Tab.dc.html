FORWARD DESIGN, STATED: the market phase has no ADR — no currency, no order wire, no books exist (ADR-024 §9 staged all three). This print is the UI half of that future ADR, grounded in what IS decided: order books live at the service layer (ADR-025), fees are the staged sink (§9), alloys are fungible or there is no order book (§6a, verbatim), and Ionized Slurry is the first commodity (§6d). The wreck rule's posture, applied to commerce: written down now so the market ADR inherits a surface instead of inventing one mid-phase.

The screen's one idea: a market order is escrowed property, and this tab treats it exactly as CARGO treats a hold — listing debits your Bay or wallet on the spot (D-P3's debit-at-submission), a queued order cancels whole (the refinery's cancel ruling, reused), fills land while you are away (Routine toast, ×40 coalesced, D19 away log), and nothing on the screen is ever a guessed total. Prices anchor on ADR-024's value indices; every number here is that table wearing a currency sign.

§2 DESIGN CALLS
BOOKS ARE PER-STATION; PRICES TRAVEL
An order lives where its escrow lives: a sell order's goods sit in this station's market escrow (08c's container kind, upstream), so the book is a fact about a place — a region-wide book would teleport goods the transfer bus refuses to. The region board shows every station's best price read-only, with the leg's band named, because the price gap across a dangerous leg IS the hauling game — ADR-024 §9's "buy it elsewhere" revisit lands on this board. Listing requires your Bay at that station; browsing is free everywhere (remote works, the refinery's rule).
ESCROW IS VISIBLE PROPERTY
Listing debits the Bay or wallet at submission (D-P3's pattern) and the order card states the escrow in amber until the order dies. A queued order cancels whole, escrow returns untouched; filled portions are settled and stay settled. Pending fills wear the ◌ beside old values — the ledger rule holds on commerce too.
FEES ARE THE SINK, SHOWN
The fee is the market phase's currency sink (§9 staged it) and it appears as a composer line, never folded into a total — the refinery shows its refund even at zero for the same reason. Proposed: charged on fills, against proceeds, so a listing that never fills costs nothing and spam is bounded by escrow instead of fees. Rate is an owner ruling.
FLAT TAXONOMY — 08c §10 ANSWERED
Three flat categories (ORES · ALLOYS · COMMODITIES) — nine listable items do not need a tree, and a browser that opens folders to find Plates would be ceremony. The growth rule is pre-authorised, not deferred: a category past one screen of rows splits into a tree, the same shaped rule as D-P2's five-column collapse.
FUNGIBLE ONLY — THE GATE THAT MAKES A BOOK POSSIBLE
§6a said it as a design constraint ("a Ferrocite Plate is a Ferrocite Plate or the future market has no order book"); this tab enforces it as an admission rule. Instance items — Phase 2's blueprinted, quality-varying goods — never list here; they will need a contract surface, which is a different screen and is deliberately not sketched.
AWAY PARITY, INHERITED
One order can fill forty times (07d wrote the coalescing rule against exactly this screen, two phases early). Fills raise Routine toasts ×N coalesced, land in the D19 event record, and the away log totals them — the tab shows current state only and never owes a notification, the refinery's honesty at any staleness.
§3 DELIBERATELY ABSENT · OPEN FOR THE MARKET ADR
ABSENT BECAUSE THE DESIGN SAYS SO
▸ No price charts — history depth is service-layer storage nobody has priced; the 24H volume column is the honest minimum. Charts join when the stream exists.
▸ No margin/partial-escrow orders — escrow is always whole, or the ledger rule dies. Financial instruments are not Phase 3.
▸ No cross-station delivery — buying at Talis-7 puts goods in your Talis-7 Bay. The market never hauls; haulers haul.
▸ No contract surface — non-fungible trades, courier contracts and escorts are a different screen for a different phase.
OPEN — OWNER RULINGS THE MARKET ADR NEEDS
▸ The currency — name, denomination, and its faucet (mining bounties? refining? mission pay?). This print says "{{ curName }}" via a tweak because the word is not designable here.
▸ Fee point and rate — proposed on-fill against proceeds; on-listing is the anti-spam alternative and charges failures.
▸ Order lifetime — until-cancelled (shown) vs N-day expiry with escrow return. Until-cancelled plus visible escrow is self-limiting; expiry adds a timer the away log must then own.
▸ Who seeds the first book — pure player-to-player from zero, or an NPC vendor selling Ionized Slurry as the bootstrap faucet (§6d names the commodity; nothing names the vendor).
