# Oracle.exe sim scenario (design/reference-build.md section 10): a follow directive to
# Content's NPC ship (P3.6c). As `follow`, but the follower is made by Content.dll
# (`content`: FSystemInfo::CreateNpcShip through OracleSimCreateNpcShip), so its manager has
# Content's FContentNpcShip as its directive and content callback: the follow behaviour's range
# crossings reach the callback (IBehaviorManager::NotifyFollowEvent; FContentNpcShip keeps the
# empty follow_event) and the end of the directive reaches the ship's OnDirectiveComplete. The
# ship's vibe is of he_p_grp, whose faction properties the NPC ship's constructor reads, and its
# state is submitted with the default personality. Multiplayer stepping.

# rust: server Content makes the follower (content)

ship leader he_fighter li_p_he_fighter_loadout01 15000 3000 -25000 ai FIGHTER psi
