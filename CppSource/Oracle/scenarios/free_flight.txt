# Oracle.exe sim scenario (design/reference-build.md section 10): free flight.
# A fighter with no behaviour accelerates at full throttle, yaws with a fixed axis
# throttle for two seconds, and flies straight again. Single-player stepping: one
# clamped physics step per frame, as the client steps it.
#
# Open space in he01, above the trade-lane plane (lanes span y = -800..800).

system he01
ticks 300
frame 0.0333333333333333
stepping sp

ship pilot he_fighter li_p_he_fighter_loadout01 15000 3000 -25000 psi

at 1 pilot throttle 1
at 150 pilot axis 0 0.5 0
at 210 pilot axis 0 0 0
