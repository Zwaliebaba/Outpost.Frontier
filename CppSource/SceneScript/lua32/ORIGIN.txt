Copied unmodified from the tarball:
  include/lua.h, luadebug.h, lualib.h, lauxlib.h
  src/lapi.c, lstate.c, ldo.c, lundump.c, lvm.c, ltable.c, lgc.c,
  lstring.c, lobject.c, lmem.c, lfunc.c, ltm.c, lzio.c, llex.c,
  lbuiltin.c, lauxlib.c, lbuffer.c, plus matching headers / lopcodes.h
  src/lib/lmathlib.c, src/lib/liolib.c, src/lib/lstrlib.c

Not copied (baseline DllMain does not lua_strlibopen):
  src/lua/*, src/luac/*, src/lib/ldblib.c,
  src/lib/linit.c (that linit opens strlib)

lstrlib.c is compiled so liolib can link luaI_classend / luaI_singlematch.
lua_strlibopen is never called (same as baseline DllMain).

lparser_stub.c includes the shared Lua 3.2.2 parser from
CppSource/luaprofile/lua32/src/lparser.c. SceneScript uses that parser
to evaluate text scripts.

SceneScript additions (not PUC-Rio):
  lparser_stub.c — one-line #include of the shared luaprofile lparser.c
  (historically a luaY_parser-errors stub, no longer)

SceneScript alterations of PUC-Rio files (plainly marked):
  include/lua.h — LUA_VERSION "Lua 3.2"; stock 3.2.2 was "Lua 3.2.2"
  src/llex.c — added #include <stdio.h> for MSVC sprintf
